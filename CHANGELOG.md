# Changelog

## V-0.1

New maps:
- CLIMAX: the Space Tree arena from 8-3, bigger and floating in space. Every now and then the gravity flips and
  everyone ends up fighting on the ceiling.
- PIANO ROOM: the organ hall from 6-2, without Gabriel.
- BURNING ARENA: all of 4-2 without enemies. Stay off the sand, it burns. Best with 6-8 players.

Added:
- Chat in matches (T).
- Jukebox next to the weapon console in every map, with the whole soundtrack. Songs play for the whole lobby.
- The main menu shows how many players are online.
- Update notice in the main menu when a new version is out.
- MAPS page: hovering a map plays a VHS glitch and shows how many players it's made for.

Changed:
- WHITE SPACE and CHESS have one weapon console instead of two.

Not compatible with V-0.0.4, everyone in the lobby has to update.

## V-0.0.4
- Online through Epic Online Services: no Steam needed anymore, and Steam and non-Steam players can play together.
  You don't need an Epic account.
- Public and private lobbies. Every lobby has a short ID you can copy and share (JOIN BY ID).
- Profiles: pick your picture (or use your Steam one) and your name in CUSTOMIZE > PROFILE.
- Whiplash clash: when two whiplashes hit each other both players get locked. Mash whiplash to win, the loser gets
  stunned.
- The MAPS page lists the game modes: VERSUS, plus ULTRAKILL ARENA and ARMS RACE (coming soon).
- CHESS: choose who plays white and black from the console, check and checkmate are announced, and the promotion
  menu shows up in front of the pawn.
- Fixed your arm staying hidden after leaving spectator mode.
- The zip now has `EOSSDK-Win64-Shipping.dll` too. Keep it next to `Ultrakill-PVP.dll`.

## V-0.0.3
- CUSTOMIZE menu: change the colors of your V1 (body, wings and lights). Everyone in the lobby sees them.
- New MAPS page with bodycam previews of every map.
- Parried rockets, cores, cannonballs and chainsaws now always fly back properly online.
- Launched chainsaws can be parried too.
- Fixed sliding players sinking into the floor for everyone else.

## V-0.0.2.1
- Fixed damage: every hit counts now. Before, a shotgun blast only did one pellet's damage.
- Spectator camera: turn off PVP ENABLED in the pause menu to fly around or follow players.
- F1 hides the HUD. New SPECTATOR & HUD page in the pause menu.

## V-0.0.2
- Projectile parry: punch a rival's rocket, core, cannonball or chainsaw right before it hits you and it's yours.
  It can go back and forth, getting stronger every time.
- A chainsaw stuck in a player grinds for 5 seconds and then explodes. Only a Knuckleblaster punch pulls it out.
- Blood is shared, with NO BLOOD and CLEAR BLOOD options.
- Your rivals' health, style rank and damage to you on the left of the screen.
- Steam invites from the lobby panel work now, plus a STEAM INVITE button.
- CHESS (experimental): only the Museum's main courtyard is playable.
- WHITE SPACE is light grey instead of blinding white.
- F11: sparring, the mirror dummy's projectiles hurt you.
- The game opens on your main monitor.

## V-0.0.1
First release.
- PVP with every weapon and arm: headshots, parries, coins and whiplash all work against players.
- Two maps: WHITE SPACE and CHESS (experimental).
- Steam lobbies with friend invites.
- Kills and deaths scoreboard, weapon consoles in every map, and host options for health, dash and damage.
- Your save and your loadout are never touched.
- Mirror dummy to practice when you're alone (F9 places it, F10 freezes it).
